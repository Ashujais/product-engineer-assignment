Key Takeaways:
- Treat conversion forms as a dialogue, not a data-entry chore. Every extra field drops conversion by 5-10%.
- Decouple third-party integrations (CRM webhooks, transactional emails) via async worker queues.
- Never use sentinel values (-1) for error handling in typed business logic; embrace strict types and domain exceptions.
- In multi-sided platforms (like ShareAtDoorStep or Milaap), display cost/contribution expectations upfront to maximize donor trust and minimize cart abandonment.
